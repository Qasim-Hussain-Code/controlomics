# Methods development record

The implemented method is currently a representation and validation layer. Researchers provide explicit analyses and claims. Strict typed records preserve control class, scientific level, biological context, evidence states, expected behavior, failure interpretation, uncertainty, and provenance. Local parsing performs structural and relational checks. No claim extraction, candidate inference, source retrieval, or numerical computation is performed.

The intended scientific method separates candidate reasoning from verification. Retrieved publication identity does not imply entailment. Entailment does not imply context compatibility. A scientifically reviewed recommendation does not imply that execution succeeded or that the original claim is true. Each transition requires inspectable records and an explicit possibility of abstention.

The initial examples and tests are synthetic or manually specified development fixtures. Their role is to exercise representation and invalid-state rejection. They provide no biological validation or performance estimate. Empirical methods, ablations, statistical analysis, expert annotation, and limitations will be updated as work is performed. No Results section is warranted at this stage.
